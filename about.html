About

My name is Uaaron and I am a Business Analyst/Product Owner with a background in web application development and data analysis. I am driven by my fascination with the ever-evolving landscape of technology and how it can solve real-world business challenges. I specialize in eliciting and translating stakeholder requirements into actionable solutions, leveraging Agile methodologies, data-driven insights, and technical skills to deliver efficient, high-impact outcomes.

In my current role as a Business Analyst, I lead Agile project development for statewide Election Management Platforms, managing iterative delivery, facilitating stakeholder collaboration, and eliciting, prioritizing, and translating complex business requirements into clear, actionable specifications for development teams. These responsibilities have honed my adaptability, attention to detail, organizational skills, and ability to thrive in fast-paced environments, enabling me to ensure projects stay aligned with client objectives, meet timelines, and deliver reliable, high-value results.

My ultimate aim is to leverage my skills to contribute meaningfully to innovative projects and collaborative teams. I’m passionate about bridging business needs with technical solutions to create scalable, user-focused systems that drive efficiency and satisfaction. I am open to full-time opportunities, including remote roles. Outside of work, you can find me hiking the local wilderness areas or in the pool where I swim and play water polo.
